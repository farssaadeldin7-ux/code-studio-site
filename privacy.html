Privacy

What we collect, why, and what we never see. Last updated 25 October 2026.

This website

codestudioplugin.com is a static site. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The pricing page fetches the live catalogue from our billing service so displayed prices always match checkout; that request carries no personal data.

The plugins

Each Code Studio plugin runs entirely on your machine. Its only network contact is our billing service at billing.codestudioplugin.com, for licence checks and checkout. What crosses the wire: a licence key, a plugin id, a hashed device identifier, a device label (your machine's hostname) and usage counts. The billing service never sees your prompts, your files, your code or any tool input or output.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card details. For an active licence we keep: your email address, the licence key, the plugin and plan purchased, subscription status, and the device records above. We use this solely to operate licensing — never for advertising, and we do not sell or share it.

Your choices

The free tools in every plugin work without a licence and send nothing. To cancel a subscription, use the billing portal from your plugin's licence tools. To ask about or delete your data, open an issue at github.com/farssaadeldin7-ux/claude-plugin-suite.
