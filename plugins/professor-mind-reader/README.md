# Professor Mind-Reader

> Rubric analysis that reveals what graders actually prioritize.

**Category:** Academic · **Tier:** Flagship

## Overview

Professor Mind-Reader analyzes assignment rubrics, past feedback, and grading patterns to identify exactly what graders prioritize, giving students and professionals a competitive edge in graded work.

## Target audience

Students aiming for top grades; professionals in certification or graded-assessment programs.

**The pain it solves:** Effort misallocated to criteria the grader barely weighs; grade ceilings from missing implicit expectations.

## Skill required to leverage it

Rubric sourcing — users need to collect the rubric, prompt, and any past graded feedback; the analysis is only as good as the documents provided.

## Monetization

Premium subscriptions for students targeting top grades; semester-length plans aligned to academic calendars.
