# Basecamp Split

> Group logistics and shared-gear management for expeditions.

**Category:** Bushcraft · **Tier:** Flagship

## Overview

Basecamp Split manages complex logistics and shared gear for groups — who carries what, meal planning, cost splitting — built for the growing market of guided outdoor experiences.

## Target audience

Professional outdoor guides, expedition leaders, and organized trip groups.

**The pain it solves:** Logistics overhead eats guide margin; gear gaps and duplication hurt client satisfaction and safety.

## Skill required to leverage it

Trip templating — guides should encode their standard loadouts and group roles once so each new trip starts from a proven template.

## Monetization

Per-guide professional licensing; essential-tool positioning for expedition companies (reduced overhead, improved client satisfaction).
