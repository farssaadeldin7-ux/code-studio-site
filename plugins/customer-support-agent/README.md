# AI-Powered Customer Support Agent

> Ticket resolution trained on your own knowledge base.

**Category:** Customer Support · **Tier:** Professional

## Overview

An AI support agent that resolves customer tickets from a structured knowledge base, escalating what it cannot answer instead of hallucinating.

## Target audience

E-commerce managers and SaaS founders.

**The pain it solves:** Support volume scales faster than headcount; slow responses churn customers.

## Skill required to leverage it

Knowledge Base Architecture — the ability to structure FAQs and manuals in a clear, logical hierarchy is essential for training the AI to handle complex tickets without hallucinating.

## Monetization

Per-resolved-ticket pricing with a monthly platform fee; ROI framed against support headcount.

## Roadmap

- [ ] KB ingestion and hierarchy linter
- [ ] Confidence-gated escalation to humans
- [ ] Helpdesk integrations (Zendesk, Gorgias, Intercom)
