# Diagnose by Sound

> Automated audio diagnostics for engines and machinery.

**Category:** Mechanical · **Tier:** Flagship

## Overview

Diagnose by Sound listens to a recording of a running engine or machine and identifies likely mechanical faults from its acoustic signature. It automates the diagnostic process for mechanics and DIY enthusiasts, cutting hours off troubleshooting time.

## Target audience

Professional mechanics, repair shops, and DIY enthusiasts.

**The pain it solves:** Hours lost to manual troubleshooting; slow turnaround limits shop throughput.

## Skill required to leverage it

Baseline mechanical literacy — users should capture clean audio samples and know the difference between idle, load, and cold-start conditions so the model gets comparable input.

## Monetization

Highest immediate ROI of the portfolio. Per-seat licensing for professional shops (faster turnaround = higher throughput), with a free tier for DIY users as a funnel.
