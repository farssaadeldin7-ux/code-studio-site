# Mental-Health Chatbot

> Supportive conversations with built-in escalation to humans.

**Category:** Health & Wellness · **Tier:** Professional

## Overview

A support-focused (not crisis-intervention) conversational tool for wellness programs, with hard escalation paths to human professionals.

## Target audience

Telehealth platforms and HR wellness departments.

**The pain it solves:** Wellness demand outstrips clinician availability; unsupported employees disengage or churn.

## Skill required to leverage it

Clinical Triage Protocols — operators must be proficient in recognizing when to escalate a conversation to a human professional; the tool is for support, not crisis intervention.

## Monetization

B2B platform licensing (per-covered-employee for HR; per-active-user for telehealth). Compliance posture is the moat.

## Roadmap

- [ ] Configurable escalation rules and audit log
- [ ] Crisis-keyword hard handoff (always-on, non-configurable)
- [ ] Anonymized program-level wellness reporting
