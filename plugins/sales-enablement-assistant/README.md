# Sales Enablement Assistant

> Personalized outreach that doesn't read like a template.

**Category:** Sales · **Tier:** Professional

## Overview

Drafts personalized prospecting emails and call prep from CRM context, tuned by the user to keep a human tone.

## Target audience

B2B sales teams and account executives.

**The pain it solves:** Reps spend selling hours on research and drafting; generic outreach kills reply rates.

## Skill required to leverage it

Prospecting Psychology — users must understand how to refine the AI's output to maintain a human tone, ensuring personalized emails don't sound like generic templates.

## Monetization

Per-seat pricing for sales teams; value framed against reply-rate lift and rep time recovered.

## Roadmap

- [ ] CRM context ingestion (HubSpot, Salesforce)
- [ ] Tone profiles per rep
- [ ] Sequence-aware follow-up drafting
