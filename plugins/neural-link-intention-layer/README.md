# Neural-Link Intention Layer

> Predictive tool-switching that anticipates your next action.

**Category:** Creative Tools · **Tier:** Professional

## Overview

Learns an artist's repetitive tool-switching and hotkey patterns and pre-stages the next action, shrinking the gap between intention and execution.

## Target audience

Professional digital artists and UI/UX designers.

**The pain it solves:** Thousands of daily micro-interruptions from tool switching compound into lost flow and billable hours.

## Skill required to leverage it

Advanced Workflow Optimization — artists need to understand their own repetitive hotkey patterns to effectively configure the plugin's predictive settings.

## Monetization

Pro-tool pricing (monthly per seat), positioned like a hardware upgrade: measurable minutes-saved-per-day metric in-app.
