# Real-Time Emotional Resonance Analyzer

> See predicted audience emotion across your timeline.

**Category:** Video Editing · **Tier:** Professional

## Overview

Overlays a predicted audience-emotion graph on the edit timeline so editors can see where resonance builds and where drop-off risk spikes.

## Target audience

Professional video editors and documentary filmmakers.

**The pain it solves:** Pacing problems surface only in test screenings or post-release analytics — after the edit is locked.

## Skill required to leverage it

Narrative Pacing Theory — editors need to understand the why behind audience drop-off to interpret the graph and make the correct creative adjustments.

## Monetization

NLE plugin subscription (Premiere, Resolve, Final Cut); project-based pricing for documentary productions.
